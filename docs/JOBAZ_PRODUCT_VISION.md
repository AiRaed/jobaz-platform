# JobAZ Product Vision

JobAZ is a **practical UK career route platform**.

It helps people understand their goal, get a UK career route, see work they can start now, find the training or licences they need next, improve their CV, and apply with confidence.

## What JobAZ is (and is not)

JobAZ is **not only** a jobs site, course site, or CV tool.

It connects those pieces into one practical journey so users know:

1. What they are aiming for
2. What work they can start now
3. What training or licence comes next
4. How to improve their CV for that route
5. How to apply with clearer next steps

## Core user journey

```
Homepage → Career Assistant → My Plan → Courses/Licences → Documents/CV → Jobs/Opportunities
```

**My Plan** is the centre of the journey. Other tools should support the active plan, not replace it.

## Business model

### First (near-term)

- Affiliate course and licence referrals
- Training provider partnerships

JobAZ recommends useful, relevant training when it genuinely helps the user move forward — not random course lists.

### Later (longer-term)

- Job and opportunity listings
- Provider dashboards
- Premium tools
- Broader business partnerships

## Admin and AI-managed platform

JobAZ is also designed as an **AI-managed platform for a solo founder**, through:

- **Admin AI** — product advisor, technical checker, marketing assistant, affiliate scout, supervisor, task organiser
- **Site Brain** — strategic AI memory (mission, launch stage, must-not-do, priorities, risks)

Admin AI suggests. The founder decides.

## Product principles

- Clear, practical, UK-focused, honest — no hype and no false promises
- Do not invent providers, prices, jobs, income, or visa guarantees
- Prefer published courses with real referral or official URLs
- Recommendation-only courses must not show Apply Now
- Preserve reusable business and route knowledge in the Knowledge Layer
